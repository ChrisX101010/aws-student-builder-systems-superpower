01 // About Me

Economics taught me to think in systems. Engineering lets me build them.

I am a University of Belgrade student focused on systems and software engineering. My path crosses business, full-stack development, cloud infrastructure and low-level engineering, which gives me a practical way to reason about both technical systems and the outcomes they are meant to produce.

I am especially interested in deterministic runtimes, compiler/toolchain work, distributed systems, reliability and performance. I prefer projects where claims can be tested: a benchmark, a reproducible failure, a stable invariant, a public contribution or a working deployment.
